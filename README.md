# Side-B
This game is in the POV of a 16 year old girl: Charlie

6 Months after her 19 year old sister Kim passed away, she finally stepped into Kim's bedroom to help pack up her remaining stuff. For those 6 months, Charlie carried the guilt of surviving while her sister didn't. She believes that a hallway argument she had with Kim the night before the incident was the reason all of this happened. She unravels a set of clues that reveal the truth and bounces back emotionally...

All voice clips were created using Fish Audio.
The room was rendered in Fusion, and other models were found online.
